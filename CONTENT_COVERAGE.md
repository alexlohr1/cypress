## Items intentionally not fabricated
The public Angular shell does not expose every dynamic child URL to static crawling. Where the exact current destination could not be recovered reliably, the prototype preserves the item and routes to an official Cypress/M-DCPS source rather than inventing a URL. Personnel should be validated against the live roster before production.
