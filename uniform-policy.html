Uniform Policy

Cypress uniform requirements presented as accessible web content, with the official M-DCPS PDF retained as the source document.

Elementary (PK–5)

Tops: Navy or gold polo-style shirts with the Cypress logo.

Bottoms: Navy or khaki pants, skorts, or Bermuda shorts.

Middle School

Tops: Red or black polo-style shirts with the Cypress logo.

Bottoms: Khaki or black pants or Bermuda shorts.

PE: Cypress/PTSA PE T-shirt and black basketball shorts.

All Students

Only approved school spirit/Friday T-shirts are allowed. Closed shoes or sneakers are required. School ID badges must be worn.

Outerwear

Sweatshirts, sweaters, and jackets must be solid color. Only Cypress-approved middle-school hoodies are allowed.

Daily uniform inspections may be conducted. The official policy also identifies unacceptable apparel and parent responsibilities. Use the source PDF for the complete controlling language.
