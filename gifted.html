Gifted Program

Cypress offers part-time and full-time gifted options designed for diverse, high-achieving learners.

Enriched learning

Challenge, creativity, and real-world application

Students engage in an enriched and rigorous curriculum that promotes critical thinking, creativity, problem-solving, and a love of learning while preparing them for advanced coursework.

Differentiated Instruction

Curriculum designed to challenge advanced learners through depth, complexity, and creativity.

Critical Thinking & Problem-Solving

Real-world scenarios foster innovation, analysis, and higher-order thinking skills.

Project-Based Learning

Hands-on, inquiry-driven projects connect academic concepts to real-life applications.

Collaboration & Leadership

Students work with peers who share similar abilities while building teamwork, confidence, and communication.

Enrichment Beyond the Core

Exposure to advanced content in STEM, humanities, arts, and global studies.

Preparation for the Future

Builds independence, resilience, and academic excellence for honors and advanced coursework.
