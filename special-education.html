Special Education

Cypress K-8 Center's ESE program is built on the belief that every student can achieve success when provided with the right supports.

Inclusive & student-centered

Individualized support that helps students thrive

Through purposeful scaffolding, tailored services, and individualized accommodations, Cypress supports students in developing academic, social, and self-advocacy skills while working closely with families and staff.

MTSS Process

Multi-Tiered System of Supports helps match students with appropriate academic, behavioral, and emotional support through ongoing data review and collaboration.

Behavior Supports

Functional Assessments of Behavior (FABs) and individualized Behavior Intervention Plans (BIPs) support positive behaviors and student success across settings.

RBT & ABA Collaboration

Registered Behavior Technicians and Applied Behavior Analysis professionals are welcomed as partners in supporting student growth and implementing behavior plans effectively.

Individualized Education Plans

The ESE team develops and implements customized IEPs aligned with each student's learning needs, strengths, goals, services, and supports.

Section 504 Plans

Qualifying students receive tailored supports and accommodations to ensure equal access to instruction, activities, and school resources.
